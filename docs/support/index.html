Common questions

Speakeasy can't find my speaker

  • Make sure your iPhone and the speaker are on the same Wi-Fi network (the speaker can be wired to the same network).
  • Allow Local Network access: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › Speakeasy. Speakeasy talks to the speaker directly on your network and can't work without it.
  • Turn the speaker on at the wall and wait for it to join the network, then pull down on the speaker list to scan again.
  • Still nothing? Tap the speaker name at the top of the remote, choose Manual IP Address and type the speaker's address (shown in your router's list of devices).

Which speakers work?

TestedKEF LSX II (firmware 3.1.141), with a KC62 subwoofer
Should workLSX II LT, LS50 Wireless II and LS60 Wireless, which use the same platform and network API
Not supportedThe original LSX and LS50 Wireless, which use a different protocol

Do I need KEF's app?

Not if your speaker is plugged into your router with an Ethernet cable. A wired speaker joins your network by itself, Speakeasy finds it, and settings and firmware updates are in Speakeasy too. To put a speaker on Wi-Fi without a cable, set it up once in KEF Connect; after that, Speakeasy handles everyday control and settings.

Widgets, Control Center and Siri say they can't reach the speaker

They use the speaker Speakeasy connected to last, so open the app once to choose your speaker. If you turned off Local Network access, turn it back on as above. Volume changes from widgets and Siri never go above the speaker's own volume limit.

Firmware updates

Speakeasy shows the installed firmware and can check for updates. An update only starts after you confirm it, and the speaker restarts afterwards. KEF's own app and the speaker's automatic updates work as before.

Is Speakeasy made by KEF?

No. Speakeasy is an independent app that works with KEF wireless speakers. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by KEF. KEF is a trademark of its owner.
