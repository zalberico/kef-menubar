Privacy Policy

Speakeasy for iPhone and KEF Menu Bar for Mac.

Home Support Privacy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

The apps collect no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. They talk to your speaker on your local network, and that's it.

What the apps do

  • Your speaker, on your network. The apps find KEF speakers on your local network and send commands straight to them: volume, input, power, sound and speaker settings. This traffic stays on your network and never goes through a server of ours. There is no server of ours.
  • Album art. When the speaker reports album art for what's playing, the apps load the picture from the address the speaker gives. That address usually belongs to the streaming service you are using, so that service sees an ordinary image request, as it would from its own app.
  • Settings on your device. Your chosen speaker, its address and your preferences are stored on your device, shared only between the app and its own widgets. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Updates for the Mac app. The KEF Menu Bar app downloaded from GitHub can check GitHub for new versions. It asks before checking on its own, and sends nothing beyond a normal web request (no system profile). App Store versions are updated by the App Store.

What the apps don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs, and no third-party SDKs on iPhone.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No sale or sharing of data, because no data is collected.

Permissions

Local Network is the only permission the iPhone app asks for. It's needed to find and control your speaker. You can turn it off any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

Children

The apps collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub, or see Support.
